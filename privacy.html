Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Felicity Capital Limited Liability Company ("Felicity Capital", "we", "us") collects, how we use it and the choices you have. It covers this website and our real estate business, including the calls, text messages and e-mails we exchange with real estate agents, property owners and others we do business with. It does not cover the Mared, Wusta or Learno products.

Information we collect

  • Contact details. Your name, your company or brokerage, your phone number and e-mail address, and the property you are connected with. We get these from public property listings, from you when you contact us, and from people who refer you to us.
  • Our communications with you. Records of calls, voicemails you leave us, text messages and e-mails between you and us, and your contact preferences, including any request to stop. We record a call only after telling you and getting your agreement.
  • Website data. This website has no accounts, forms or advertising cookies. When a page loads, the companies that host it and serve its fonts receive standard technical data such as your IP address and browser type.

How we use it

  • To contact you about buying or selling a property, and to answer you.
  • To evaluate, negotiate and complete real estate transactions.
  • To keep records of our dealings and honor your contact preferences.
  • To meet our legal obligations and protect our rights.

Text messages

We send text messages about specific properties and transactions: questions about a listing, scheduling, and offers. Message frequency varies with the conversation. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts from us, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. The only exception is the service providers that deliver our messages, who may use this information solely to provide that service to us.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only:

  • with service providers that work for us, such as our phone and messaging carrier and our data hosting provider, who may use it only to provide their services to us;
  • with the parties needed to complete a transaction you are part of, such as a title company or closing attorney;
  • when the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information for as long as we need it for the purposes above and to keep the business and legal records we are required to keep. A request to stop contacting you is kept so that we can keep honoring it.

Your choices

  • Texts: reply STOP to any text from us.
  • Calls and e-mail: tell us on a call or by e-mail and we will stop.
  • Your information: e-mail us to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We will answer within 30 days.

Children

Our website and services are for adults doing business with us. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
